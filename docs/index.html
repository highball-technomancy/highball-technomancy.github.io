What we believe

01

“What if?” is reason enough.

We're out to do interesting things and see what can be done. When an idea grabs us, we chase it to see what's on the other side — treasure or disappointment.

02

Nothing grades its own homework.

AI is our favorite problem. Working with it has taught us that confidence isn't proof, and a convincing answer can still miss what mattered. So whatever does the work never gets the last word on it.

03

We toast together.

We work better together. Between people; between machines; between the blurring lines of biological and synthetic life. Some of our work is alive, while the rest is design docs, strong opinions, and the scribblings of madmen. We share the napkin blueprints as readily as the finished work. There's room for other minds while an idea is taking shape. It's more fun that way.
